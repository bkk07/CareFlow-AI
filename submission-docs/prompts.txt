Platform Admin:

Hospital Admin:

- manage their hospital

Doctor:

- access their own provider data
- manage their own availability/calendar
- view authorized appointments
- view authorized questionnaire responses

Patient:

- manage their own profile
- access their own appointments
- access their own questionnaires
- manage their own preferences
- perform allowed booking/rescheduling/cancellation operations

OPERATIONS ADMIN

If the repository already has an operations-admin role, define its permissions explicitly for:

- reconciliation
- workflow operations
- integration failures
- operational monitoring
- retry/resolve operations

Do not automatically give operations-admin unrestricted platform-admin access.

ROUTER AUTHORIZATION

Protected routers should use centralized authorization.

Avoid repeating raw role strings and authorization logic throughout route handlers.

Prefer:

router
    ↓
authentication context
    ↓
require_role(...)
    ↓
service

RESOURCE AUTHORIZATION

Role authorization alone is insufficient.

For example:

A hospital admin may have permission to update doctors, but only doctors belonging to their own hospital.

A doctor may access appointments, but only appointments they are authorized to see.

A patient may access appointments, but only their own appointments.

Implement ownership/resource checks through services or shared authorization helpers where appropriate.

Do not authorize a request merely because the request body contains:

hospital_id = current user's hospital

The server must derive or validate tenant ownership from trusted authenticated context and database relationships.
